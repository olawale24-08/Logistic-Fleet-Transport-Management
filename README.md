# Logistics & Fleet Transport Management Analytics

## Overview

This project presents an end-to-end data analytics solution for analyzing logistics and fleet transportation operations.

The objective was to transform raw transportation data into actionable business insights by evaluating revenue, delivery performance, fuel efficiency, vehicle utilization, maintenance costs, and customer satisfaction.

The project covers the complete analytics workflow, from dataset loading and cleaning to exploratory data analysis, interactive Power BI dashboard development, and business reporting.

## Business Problem

Logistics companies generate large volumes of operational data across vehicles, drivers, deliveries, fuel consumption, maintenance, and customer interactions.

Without effective analysis, it can be difficult to:

* Monitor transportation costs and revenue.
* Identify fuel efficiency trends.
* Evaluate vehicle utilization.
* Track maintenance expenses.
* Understand customer satisfaction across regions.
* Identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency.

This project addresses these challenges by creating an interactive analytics dashboard for monitoring key logistics performance indicators.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze revenue and delivery performance.
* Evaluate fuel consumption and efficiency trends.
* Compare transportation costs across periods and regions.
* Examine vehicle utilization by truck type.
* Analyze maintenance costs and operational expenses.
* Understand customer satisfaction across states.
* Present findings through interactive dashboards and a supporting analytical report.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Loading

Imported the raw logistics dataset into the analytics environment and reviewed the available tables, columns, and data types.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Transformation

Performed data preparation activities, including:

* Checking for missing and inconsistent values.
* Removing duplicate records where applicable.
* Correcting data types.
* Standardizing categorical fields.
* Preparing date-related fields for time-series analysis.
* Validating numerical and financial data.
* Creating a structured dataset suitable for reporting.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Analyzed the dataset to identify trends, relationships, and operational patterns.

Key areas explored:

* Revenue and delivery trends over time.
* Fuel efficiency by month and year.
* Delivery cost patterns.
* Maintenance expenses by truck type.
* Vehicle utilization across fleet categories.
* Revenue distribution by state.
* Customer satisfaction across regions.

### 4. Data Modeling & DAX

Created analytical measures and supporting calculations to track important business KPIs.

Examples include:

* Total Revenue
* Total Costs
* Cost per Kilometer
* Cost per Delivery
* Average Delivery Distance
* Total Deliveries
* Fuel Efficiency
* Maintenance Cost
* Vehicle Utilization

### 5. Power BI Dashboard Development

Designed an interactive dashboard with filters, KPI cards, charts, and visual comparisons to make logistics performance easier to understand.

### 6. Analytical Reporting

Prepared a supporting report documenting the project methodology, key findings, performance trends, and potential business improvement opportunities.
